<p>Stephen K. Lucas, Evelyn Sander (of the GMU Math Makerlab), and Laura Taalman wrote an article about how to practically 3D print actual physical models of such dynamical structures. This appeared in an <a href="https://www.ams.org/notices/202011/rnoti-p1692.pdf">article in the American Mathematical Society Notices in December 2020</a> (cover image shown is a 3D printed model from the article). An adapted version appeared in the book <a href="https://press.princeton.edu/books/paperback/9780691225708/the-best-writing-on-mathematics-2021">The Best Writing on Mathematics 2021, Princeton University Press</a>.</p>
